# 5. FOLLOW THE CREATIVE AMBITION OF THE APPLE DESIGN AWARDS

Use Apple's official design guidance and the Apple Design Awards as benchmarks for the entire portfolio.

Evaluate every application through the six recognized Apple Design Awards dimensions:

**Delight and Fun:** Does the application produce satisfaction, enjoyment, and memorable experiences appropriate to its purpose?

**Inclusivity:** Is the experience accessible, understandable, and thoughtfully designed for different users?

**Innovation:** Does the product introduce meaningful improvements rather than repeating conventional software patterns?

**Interaction:** Are gestures, controls, navigation, animations, and feedback exceptionally intuitive and responsive?

**Social Impact:** Where relevant, does the application contribute meaningful benefits to users or society?

**Visuals and Graphics:** Does every screen demonstrate outstanding typography, visual composition, imagery, animation, and graphical craftsmanship?

Apply these dimensions as internal quality standards.

Do not claim that following them guarantees an actual Apple Design Award.

**EVERY APPLICATION MUST BE DESIGNED WITH THE AMBITION OF BECOMING AN INDEPENDENT APPLE DESIGN AWARD CONTENDER.**
