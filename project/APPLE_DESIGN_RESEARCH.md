# Official design research — 17 November 2026

This is a design study, not an Apple endorsement, award prediction or device verification. Principles inform original solutions; no referenced app screens, logos or artwork are copied.

| Primary source consulted | Finding | Product decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/) and [design principles](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles/) | Purpose, agency, responsibility, familiarity and flexibility guide competing design decisions. | Put the domain task first; expose evidence and recovery paths; retain recognizable navigation. |
| [WWDC26: Communicate your brand identity on iOS](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/251/) | Brand expression belongs strongly in content; platform navigation should remain understandable. The session discusses typography, color, iconography and flat hierarchies. | Replace the identical symbol/name masthead with independent product signatures, rather than making 25 obscure navigation mechanisms. |
| [WWDC26: Principles of great design](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/250/) | Features consume attention and trust; focus on meaningful user choices and recoverable mistakes. | Keep public-source value available immediately; do not manufacture agent progress or require a workspace before reading. |
| [2024 awards and finalists](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024/) | Bears Gratitude connects emotional warmth with repeated use; oko demonstrates accessibility as core interaction. | Distinguish reassurance in Nafas from evidence precision in Himam. Neither needs copied characters or safety claims. |
| [2025 awards and finalists](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/) | CapWords connects a concrete action with immediate learning; Lumy organizes celestial information around a focused purpose. | A signature interaction must do useful work: document reconciliation, constraint matching or a local timer, not merely play a decorative animation. |
| [2026 awards and finalists](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/) | Moonlitt wins Interaction; Tide Guide wins Visuals and Graphics. Their information, platform behavior and domain visualization reinforce one another. | Use chronology for Adl and normalized period comparison for Amanah Eco. Do not give unrelated products interchangeable AI panels. |
| [Apple apps](https://www.apple.com/apps/) | Journaling, maps, photos, health, music and credentials organize different objects and tasks. | Organize the portfolio around clauses, dossiers, manifests, receipts, routes and source folios, not a universal chat dashboard. |
| [Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography/) | Custom typography must remain readable, scale with user settings and preserve hierarchy. | Bundled licensed display faces, individual proportions and native text; do not flatten the interface into images or constrain body text to a fixed logo box. |
| [Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility/) | Information must remain perceivable and adaptable across abilities and input methods. | Semantic labels, text status, contrast checks, multiline scaling and reduced motion; physical screen-reader testing remains open. |
| [Android system bars](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/foundations/system-bars) | Insets, cutouts, keyboard and system navigation are part of the layout. | Keep Android back behavior and safe insets; browser screenshots do not certify these native details. |

The motion Markdown endpoint was unavailable through the research reader. No claim is made to have reviewed an unavailable document. Existing reduced-motion behavior and the WWDC interaction guidance inform this iteration; full native animation and haptic assessment remains open.

## Application to this repository

The pre-change audit found a repeated 28-point outline symbol beside the same 16-point bold product name across all 25 homes. Twenty-five licensed headline families had been added, but most titles still followed one size rule and the rest of the UI retained extensive shared composition. Seven case-form families and five navigation families were not 25 independent experiences. The current work starts with the branding layer, then addresses domain-specific case interactions and the portfolio comparison. Mechanical uniqueness does not establish creative success.

Each individual direction in [creative-directions](creative-directions) defines fourteen decisions and a domain-specific functional boundary. Implementation status must be evaluated separately from those intended directions.
