# Manzil AI — independent creative direction

Status: authored direction; implementation evidence and acceptance tracked separately.

## 1. Brand philosophy

The household deserves a readable book

## 2. Emotional identity

Domestic ease and control

## 3. Logo and wordmark

An open ledger roof with a warm offset window; intimate handwritten-feeling book title

## 4. Typography

literata for the wordmark and key headlines. Wordmark 36 pt, tracking -0.7; headline 42 pt with 1.16 leading ratio. Body 18/28 using the platform reading face. Preserve system scaling, multiline content and readable fallback; never shrink body text to fit.

## 5. Color system

Paper #FFF8E9, ink #344C32, accent #507447, secondary surface #FFFFFF. Status is also expressed in words, not color alone.

## 6. Visual language

receipt composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Household book → receipt intake → duplicate register → spending categories

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Bring receipts into the ledger, flag duplicates, then reconcile

## 9. Motion identity

Receipt insertion unfolds from its point of entry This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

The agent separates duplicate, unverified and counted receipts. VAT overview does not establish the recoverability of a household receipt.

## 11. Signature experience

A repeated identifier is shown before it can inflate totals

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

housebook brand composition; Household book → receipt intake → duplicate register → spending categories. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: VAT overview does not establish the recoverability of a household receipt.
