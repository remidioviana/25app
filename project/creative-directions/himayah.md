# Himayah AI — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

The clause is the interface

## 2. Emotional identity

Reassurance through traceable precision

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Precision-cut H wordmark; an original negative-space document-fold emblem. IBM Plex Sans with IBM Plex Sans Arabic.

## 4. Typography

IBM Plex Sans for Latin branding and IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Midnight indigo `#151E37`, limestone `#F4E7D5`, signal coral `#E7655A`. Forensic document imagery and clause-level annotation. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

docket composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Clause manuscript → obligation margin → exact cited passage → revision history

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Select a clause, then expose only its obligations and source support

## 9. Motion identity

A narrow margin unfolds; no dramatic approval flourish This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Compliance X-Ray:** identifies potentially problematic clauses, retrieves authoritative regulations, presents cited findings, and prepares a reviewable remediation plan. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

A disputed sentence opens its exact supporting passage

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

docket brand composition; Clause manuscript → obligation margin → exact cited passage → revision history. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: Never turn a model opinion into legal certification.
