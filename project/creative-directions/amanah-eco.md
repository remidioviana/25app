# Amanah Eco — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Compare periods before calling it savings

## 2. Emotional identity

Agency through honest measurement

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Environmental wordmark with an original branching energy-path symbol. Raleway with Lemonada.

## 4. Typography

Raleway for Latin branding and Lemonada for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Forest green `#215B39`, pale citron `#F8F8DA`, lime `#BDD652`. Organic energy-flow visuals and precise environmental graphics. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

resource composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Resource observatory → period comparison → measurement basis → conservation reading

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Enter period lengths alongside readings so comparisons stay normalized

## 9. Motion identity

A comparison changes only when actual measurements change This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Energy Optimization Agent:** evaluates available usage data, identifies efficiency opportunities, and prepares recommendations with explicitly stated assumptions. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

A lower total over fewer days cannot masquerade as savings

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

observatory brand composition; Resource observatory → period comparison → measurement basis → conservation reading. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No invented meter connection, tariff, emissions or weather adjustment.
