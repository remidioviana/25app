# Iqamah AI — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Make a life decision legible

## 2. Emotional identity

Dignity, patience and possibility

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Refined architectural wordmark; asymmetric gateway emblem. Cormorant Garamond paired with Noto Naskh Arabic.

## 4. Typography

Cormorant Garamond for Latin branding and Noto Naskh Arabic for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Burgundy `#502C43`, warm ivory `#F7EBD7`, antique gold `#C29A5C`. Architectural editorial photography and dossier-inspired layouts. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

passport composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Route passport → requirements register → documents shelf → dated route changes

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Compare what a route asks for against documents actually held

## 9. Motion identity

Page-edge reveals establish progression without implying a government decision This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Visa Dossier Builder:** evaluates available eligibility criteria, identifies missing evidence, and assembles an application checklist using current official requirements. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

The dossier can show a missing document without an invented eligibility score

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

passport brand composition; Route passport → requirements register → documents shelf → dated route changes. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: Avoid gold badges that resemble government endorsement.
