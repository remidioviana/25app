# Sawm AI — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

A rhythm of intention and rest

## 2. Emotional identity

Calm without pressure

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Refined astronomical-inspired logotype; abstract solar-cycle mark. Newsreader with Scheherazade New.

## 4. Typography

Newsreader for Latin branding and Scheherazade New for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Night blue `#131F4D`, candlelight `#FFE8B0`, muted gold `#D0B87C`. Elegant sky gradients and astronomical visualizations. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

ritual composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Daily intention → protected rest → commitments → gentler plan

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Prioritize boundaries and rest rather than a productivity score

## 9. Motion identity

Slow reveals stop immediately under reduced motion This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Ramadan Planning Agent:** retrieves location-relevant schedules when permitted, helps organize personal fasting plans, and identifies timing-sensitive reminders. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

Rest remains protected even when the task list cannot all fit

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

dusk brand composition; Daily intention → protected rest → commitments → gentler plan. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No medical fasting advice or fabricated prayer timings.
