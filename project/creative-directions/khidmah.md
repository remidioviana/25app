# Khidmah AI — independent creative direction

Status: authored direction; implementation evidence and acceptance tracked separately.

## 1. Brand philosophy

A customer deserves resolution

## 2. Emotional identity

Warmth, fairness and a clear path

## 3. Logo and wordmark

A soft speech opening formed from two solid quarters; welcoming compact care lettering

## 4. Typography

outfit for the wordmark and key headlines. Wordmark 35 pt, tracking -0.7; headline 36 pt with 1.18 leading ratio. Body 18/28 using the platform reading face. Preserve system scaling, multiline content and readable fallback; never shrink body text to fit.

## 5. Color system

Paper #EAFAFD, ink #123F4C, accent #087B95, secondary surface #FFFFFF. Status is also expressed in words, not color alone.

## 6. Visual language

service composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Customer situation → policy basis → amount check → resolution explanation

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Start with the reason, then show why a policy applies

## 9. Motion identity

Gentle expansion communicates what the agent needs next This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

A resolution distinguishes customer report, merchant policy and calculation. Do not claim a refund transaction occurred.

## 11. Signature experience

A zero refund limit is respected instead of replaced by a default

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

care brand composition; Customer situation → policy basis → amount check → resolution explanation. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: Do not claim a refund transaction occurred.
