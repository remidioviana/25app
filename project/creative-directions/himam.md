# Himam AI — independent creative direction

Status: authored direction; implementation evidence and acceptance tracked separately.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Access evidence before routing

## 2. Emotional identity

Confidence and independence

## 3. Logo and wordmark

A continuous accessible turn with wide open negative space; exceptionally readable access signature

## 4. Typography

atkinsonhyperlegible for the wordmark and key headlines. Wordmark 37 pt, tracking -0.3; headline 41 pt with 1.16 leading ratio. Body 19/30 using the platform reading face. Preserve system scaling, multiline content and readable fallback; never shrink body text to fit.

## 5. Color system

Paper #E9F0FE, ink #152D5C, accent #2156B4, secondary surface #FFFFFF. Status is also expressed in words, not color alone.

## 6. Visual language

wayfinding composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Requirements first → barrier evidence → route alternatives → verification gaps

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Inspect known barriers and missing evidence before choosing a route

## 9. Motion identity

Immediate focus transitions; no motion needed to understand the route This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

The agent matches supplied access needs to documented route constraints. No live navigation, transport booking or safety guarantee.

## 11. Signature experience

An unknown barrier stays unknown instead of becoming an accessible route

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

access brand composition; Requirements first → barrier evidence → route alternatives → verification gaps. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No live navigation, transport booking or safety guarantee.
