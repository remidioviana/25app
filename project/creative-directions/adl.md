# Adl AI — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Let chronology carry the dispute

## 2. Emotional identity

Composure in a stressful situation

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Elegant legal-editorial wordmark; asymmetric balanced-plane emblem. Libre Baskerville with Amiri.

## 4. Typography

Libre Baskerville for Latin branding and Amiri for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Dark plum `#3C283B`, pale lavender `#E3D7E9`, muted rose `#BE8E9C`. Architectural linework and legal-document composition. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

casefile composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Chronology spine → event evidence → claimed amounts → case export

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Arrange events by supplied dates and distinguish allegations from receipts

## 9. Motion identity

The chronology extends vertically as evidence is added This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Property Rights Analyst:** reviews available documents and official rules, flags legal questions, and prepares source-backed summaries for human review. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

An unsupported claimed amount never becomes an established debt

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

chronicle brand composition; Chronology spine → event evidence → claimed amounts → case export. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: Do not present a model opinion as an RDC ruling.
