# Thawg AI — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

A considered table

## 2. Emotional identity

Anticipation and editorial discernment

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Distinctive culinary wordmark; bespoke plate-and-flavor mark. Bricolage Grotesque with Baloo Bhaijaan 2.

## 4. Typography

Bricolage Grotesque for Latin branding and Baloo Bhaijaan 2 for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Tomato `#D84A34`, vanilla `#FFF0D8`, Mediterranean blue `#3F80A6`. Rich original food photography and menu-centered imagery. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

menu composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Dining edit → needs filter → candidate shortlist → food safety notes

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Build a shortlist from actual supplied options and inspect exclusions

## 9. Motion identity

Measured editorial reveals; no fake live availability shimmer This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Dining Discovery Agent:** searches suitable restaurants, checks published menus and available certification evidence, and distinguishes confirmed from unverified halal claims. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

An allergen question becomes a concrete verification prompt

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

edition brand composition; Dining edit → needs filter → candidate shortlist → food safety notes. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: A shortlist is not a halal certificate or a reservation.
