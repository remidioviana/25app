# Hisad AI — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Food rescue is a clock and a match

## 2. Emotional identity

Urgency with dignity

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Distinctive harvest-inspired wordmark; custom interconnected produce-and-route emblem. Syne with Katibeh.

## 4. Typography

Syne for Latin branding and Katibeh for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Harvest red `#A23E3C`, warm cream `#FCF3E4`, golden yellow `#F0BB52`. Authentic food photography and redistribution logistics imagery. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

harvest composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Handling checkpoint → available lot → recipient capacities → dispatch reasoning

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Match actual quantity, dates and recipient constraints before proposing allocation

## 9. Motion identity

Decisive status changes follow verified records, never a fake countdown This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Food Redistribution Coordinator:** matches available donated food with eligible participating organizations where supported, prepares pickup coordination, and observes food safety and authorization requirements. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

An unsupported handling condition prevents a confident allocation

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

dispatch brand composition; Handling checkpoint → available lot → recipient capacities → dispatch reasoning. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No invented donation, delivery receipt or food-safety certification.
