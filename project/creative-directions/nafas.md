# Nafas AI — independent creative direction

Status: authored direction; implementation evidence and acceptance tracked separately.

## 1. Brand philosophy

A little room to breathe

## 2. Emotional identity

Gentleness and personal agency

## 3. Logo and wordmark

An airy open oval formed by two unequal strokes; softly spaced lowercase breath wordmark

## 4. Typography

quicksand for the wordmark and key headlines. Wordmark 46 pt, tracking 1.1; headline 45 pt with 1.2 leading ratio. Body 19/30 using the platform reading face. Preserve system scaling, multiline content and readable fallback; never shrink body text to fit.

## 5. Color system

Paper #EFF1E7, ink #3F513D, accent #4F6D43, secondary surface #FFFFFF. Status is also expressed in words, not color alone.

## 6. Visual language

breathing composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Breathing pause → preferred practices → gentle routine → evidence reading

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Start and stop a local pacing timer without an account

## 9. Motion identity

Breath pacing honors reduced motion and stops in the background This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

The agent suggests a bounded routine from stated preferences. No diagnosis or claim that the pacing pattern is a clinical protocol.

## 11. Signature experience

The breathing pause works locally without pretending to measure health

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

breath brand composition; Breathing pause → preferred practices → gentle routine → evidence reading. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No diagnosis or claim that the pacing pattern is a clinical protocol.
