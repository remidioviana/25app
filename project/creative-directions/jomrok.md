# Jomrok AI — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Documents before the border

## 2. Emotional identity

Operational precision and readiness

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Industrial condensed wordmark with a segmented cargo-path emblem. Barlow Condensed with Changa.

## 4. Typography

Barlow Condensed for Latin branding and Changa for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Electric blue `#1645AE`, industrial white `#F4F3ED`, vivid orange `#FF8A37`. Container schematics and shipment-flow imagery. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

manifest composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Manifest header → required fields register → declared valuation → duty assumptions

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Inspect omissions before arithmetic, keeping supplied rates visible

## 9. Motion identity

Fast mechanical reveals reflect document checks, never simulated clearance This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Customs Intelligence Agent:** analyzes shipment information, researches applicable requirements, identifies missing documentation, and flags classification uncertainties. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

A missing field is located in the manifest instead of buried in prose

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

manifest brand composition; Manifest header → required fields register → declared valuation → duty assumptions. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No live customs classification or border clearance claim.
