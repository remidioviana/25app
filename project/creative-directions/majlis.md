# Majlis AI — independent creative direction

Status: authored direction; implementation evidence and acceptance tracked separately.

## 1. Brand philosophy

An informed arrival

## 2. Emotional identity

Welcome, discretion and quiet confidence

## 3. Logo and wordmark

A low gathering arc above a single seat; generous host-like literary masthead

## 4. Typography

cormorant for the wordmark and key headlines. Wordmark 38 pt, tracking 0.4; headline 44 pt with 1.18 leading ratio. Body 18/29 using the platform reading face. Preserve system scaling, multiline content and readable fallback; never shrink body text to fit.

## 5. Color system

Paper #291F18, ink #F9EDDD, accent #E6B77E, secondary surface #272541. Status is also expressed in words, not color alone.

## 6. Visual language

protocol composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Invitation → onboarding book → protocol notes → arrival agenda

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Explore the welcome book at the executive’s pace

## 9. Motion identity

Measured page-like reveals encourage unhurried reading This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

The agent orders supplied onboarding tasks by dependencies. No invented company policies or completed onboarding actions.

## 11. Signature experience

A protocol note stays connected to its source or supplied task

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

invitation brand composition; Invitation → onboarding book → protocol notes → arrival agenda. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No invented company policies or completed onboarding actions.
