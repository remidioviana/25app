# Khibrat AI — independent creative direction

Status: authored direction; implementation evidence and acceptance tracked separately.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Evidence earns the match

## 2. Emotional identity

Fairness and professional attention

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Three offset competency tiles with one connecting notch; measured skills wordmark

## 4. Typography

dmsans for the wordmark and key headlines. Wordmark 32 pt, tracking -0.5; headline 44 pt with 1.05 leading ratio. Body 17/26 using the platform reading face. Preserve system scaling, multiline content and readable fallback; never shrink body text to fit.

## 5. Color system

Paper #DFEDE5, ink #123E36, accent #16675B, secondary surface #FFFFFF. Status is also expressed in words, not color alone.

## 6. Visual language

competency composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Role rubric → evidence matrix → unmatched criteria → interview prompts

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Open a criterion and inspect the exact candidate evidence attached

## 9. Motion identity

Focus moves between criterion and evidence rather than celebrating a score This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

The agent explains evidence gaps against the supplied role rubric. Avoid claims of fair hiring certification or unapproved applicant inference.

## 11. Signature experience

An unsupported competency remains unsubstantiated

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

competency brand composition; Role rubric → evidence matrix → unmatched criteria → interview prompts. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: Avoid claims of fair hiring certification or unapproved applicant inference.
