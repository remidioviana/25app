# Turath AI — independent creative direction

Status: authored direction; implementation evidence and acceptance tracked separately.

## 1. Brand philosophy

A source opens a story

## 2. Emotional identity

Curiosity, continuity and respect

## 3. Logo and wordmark

A fragmented archaeological rubbing with one restored accent; scholarly archive wordmark

## 4. Typography

spectral for the wordmark and key headlines. Wordmark 40 pt, tracking -0.4; headline 46 pt with 1.15 leading ratio. Body 18/30 using the platform reading face. Preserve system scaling, multiline content and readable fallback; never shrink body text to fit.

## 5. Color system

Paper #F4E2CA, ink #603821, accent #914B24, secondary surface #FFFFFF. Status is also expressed in words, not color alone.

## 6. Visual language

story composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Topic index → source folios → learning trail → citations

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Choose a topic and follow only the sources actually present

## 9. Motion identity

Folio reveals favor attentive reading rather than artificial age effects This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

The agent teaches with distinguishable source-backed statements. No invented heritage facts or copied museum imagery.

## 11. Signature experience

A topic without supporting material remains an open research question

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

archive brand composition; Topic index → source folios → learning trail → citations. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No invented heritage facts or copied museum imagery.
