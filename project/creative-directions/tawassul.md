# Tawassul B2B — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Earn the introduction

## 2. Emotional identity

Confidence without inflated promises

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Interlocking bridge-like letterform, sophisticated commercial identity. Sora with Vazirmatn.

## 4. Typography

Sora for Latin branding and Vazirmatn for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Malachite `#123D32`, cream `#EDE4CD`, copper `#B67F3C`. Geometric negotiation paths and editorial business photography. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

correspondence composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Account letter → need-to-product match → approved-fact drawer → outreach draft

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Select a customer need and expose only facts authorized for that account

## 9. Motion identity

A restrained letter reveal favors reading over spectacle This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Negotiation Architect:** evaluates stated objectives, researches public business context, prepares negotiation scenarios, and drafts correspondence for approval. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

A sales claim can be traced back to the approved account material

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

correspondence brand composition; Account letter → need-to-product match → approved-fact drawer → outreach draft. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No fabricated customer research or sent outreach.
