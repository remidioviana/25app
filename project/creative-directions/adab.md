# Adab AI — independent creative direction

Status: authored direction; implementation evidence and acceptance tracked separately.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Meaning has a local voice

## 2. Emotional identity

Editorial curiosity and cultural care

## 3. Logo and wordmark

A bold cut punctuation stroke above a widely tracked editorial wordmark

## 4. Typography

bodonimoda for the wordmark and key headlines. Wordmark 48 pt, tracking 3.0; headline 55 pt with 0.98 leading ratio. Body 17/27 using the platform reading face. Preserve system scaling, multiline content and readable fallback; never shrink body text to fit.

## 5. Color system

Paper #2B142E, ink #FFF1EA, accent #FFAC91, secondary surface #272541. Status is also expressed in words, not color alone.

## 6. Visual language

editorial composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Original copy → claim annotations → language alternatives → editorial boundaries

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Read the original beside proposed changes and inspect a specific claim

## 9. Motion identity

Precise replacement emphasis, with reduced-motion static highlights This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

The agent adapts language while preserving factual approval boundaries. Cultural adaptation is not permission to invent product claims.

## 11. Signature experience

A prohibited term points to the exact affected phrase

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

imprint brand composition; Original copy → claim annotations → language alternatives → editorial boundaries. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: Cultural adaptation is not permission to invent product claims.
