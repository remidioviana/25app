# Istithmar AI — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Make alternatives comparable

## 2. Emotional identity

Ambition grounded in explicit costs

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Modular geometric logotype inspired by interconnected economic zones. Space Grotesk with Alexandria.

## 4. Typography

Space Grotesk for Latin branding and Alexandria for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Deep blue `#133C67`, pale mint `#DDF8ED`, turquoise `#60C6AC`. Spatial diagrams and precise economic visualizations. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

decision composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Activity brief → side-by-side jurisdiction columns → cost horizon → scenario provenance

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Change horizon and inspect which supplied costs change the ordering

## 9. Motion identity

Short horizontal shifts connect a changed assumption to its affected column This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Investment Navigator:** researches applicable zones, licensing requirements, and tax rules, then prepares sourced decision comparisons. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

A zero-cost or zero-visa assumption remains an explicit zero

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

exchange brand composition; Activity brief → side-by-side jurisdiction columns → cost horizon → scenario provenance. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: Do not invent current fees or silently favor the cheapest unsupported option.
