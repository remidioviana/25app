# Tadweer AI — independent creative direction

Status: aligned to the mandatory user matrix; implementation scope and native acceptance remain separate.

## 1. Brand philosophy

Give a material its next route

## 2. Emotional identity

Practical optimism and responsibility

## 3. Logo and wordmark

Angular circular-economy wordmark with a distinctive material-loop symbol. Work Sans with Jomhuria.

## 4. Typography

Work Sans for Latin branding and Jomhuria for Arabic input. Both are bundled under retained OFL notices. Coverage and shaping evidence is separate from native screen-reader and font-scaling acceptance.

## 5. Color system

Burnt orange `#DB5A27`, recycled-paper cream `#F7F2E3`, dark petrol `#1F3942`. Material close-ups and photographic object classification. Accessible text/action tones are recorded separately; the requested signal colors remain the art-direction basis.

## 6. Visual language

circular composition, individual original artwork, and the mark geometry described above. Decorative imagery never stands in for verified output.

## 7. Information architecture

Material selection → hazardous status → supplied routes → handling context

## 8. Interaction philosophy

Identify material and handling constraints before ranking routes

## 9. Motion identity

A folded edge opens to explain the selected route This describes the product target; current implementation and device acceptance must be checked separately.

## 10. Agentic AI experience

**Recycling Identification Agent:** interprets user-submitted images, identifies probable material categories, and researches applicable disposal instructions. This is the target specification. Implemented scope and unavailable integrations are listed in the design-matrix delivery ledger.

## 11. Signature experience

A hazardous item cannot quietly enter an ordinary collection route

## 12. Platform adaptation

iOS: safe areas, system text scaling, native stack back and accessible modal dismissal. Android: system back, keyboard insets, 48 dp actions and no dependence on an iOS-only gesture. Both: reduced motion and screen-reader labels. Physical-device verification remains open.

## 13. Differentiation evidence

material brand composition; Material selection → hazardous status → supplied routes → handling context. Compare all four corresponding screens against the portfolio, not just the hero image. Similarity is a review trigger, never proof of creative success.

## 14. Implementation feasibility

Bundled licensed font, independent vector identity, static route content and actual schema-bound inputs. Reuse data validation and authentication without forcing this content into a shared dashboard. Functional boundary: No booked pickup or fabricated local collection capacity.
