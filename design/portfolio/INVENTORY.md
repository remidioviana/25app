## Differentiation guardrails

Keep procurement deadline-driven, legal review evidence-driven, budgeting transaction-driven, mobility segment-driven and wellbeing private/reflection-driven. Sharing neutral technical primitives is acceptable; copying Himayah’s dossier navigation into all 25 products is not. Existing accents cluster in greens/blues; each future unit must resolve typography, density, composition and motion independently before its visual identity is marked complete.

## Program-wide feasibility

All 25 remain free to the end user, without ads, subscriptions, purchases or required app registration. External-service authorization is contextual and optional for that operation. An operating budget or a validated on-device strategy is still needed. The current token/server setup is a development/operator mechanism, not finished accountless consumer onboarding. No 750-screen completion, signed binaries, device verification, regulatory certification or award competitiveness is claimed.
